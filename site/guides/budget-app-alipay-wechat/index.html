Money in two countries

A Mac budgeting app that imports Alipay and WeChat Pay

Updated 2026-11-22

Keel is a free, open-source personal finance app for the Mac that reads statements from US banks and brokers and from Alipay, WeChat Pay, Ningbo Bank and OKX, and books them all into one double-entry ledger. It treats card payments into Alipay or WeChat, and transfers between your own wallets, as transfers rather than spending, so the month adds up. Popular US budgeting apps connect to US institutions and do not read Chinese payment platforms. Keel's data stays on your Mac.

Why this is hard elsewhere

  • Two sets of formats. Alipay and WeChat Pay exports look nothing like a Chase CSV. Keel has a dedicated parser for each, and learns any other bank's layout after you teach it once.
  • Money moving between your own accounts. Paying your Alipay balance with a US card, topping up WeChat, or buying crypto on OKX is not spending. Keel books these as transfers, using the names you tell it are you.
  • Two currencies. One switch shows everything in USD or CNY, while account details stay in their own currency.

What you get

  • Now: this month against a usual month, a month-end forecast, what you can still spend per day, and a cash timeline to past your next payday.
  • Review: each closed month's income, spending, savings and net worth, with categories compared to usual.
  • Ledger: every transaction, a calendar of each day with upcoming bills and paydays, and edits that survive every re-import.
  • Plan: retirement and market-crash simulations on data back to 1871, allocation, backtests, mortgage and rent-or-buy.
  • Alerts: overspending pace, a paycheck that didn't arrive, cash below your floor, bills this week.
  • For agents: a keel command and an MCP server with 20 tools, so Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can check balances, categorise and plan.

FAQ

Can a Mac budgeting app read Alipay and WeChat Pay statements?

Keel can. Drop the exported statements in and it books them alongside your US accounts.

Do I have to give Keel my bank password?

No. You import statements; nothing logs in to your bank.

Is Keel free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0, with no account and no subscription.

Where is my financial data stored?

On your Mac only. Keel keeps nightly verified backups, optionally in your own iCloud Drive.
