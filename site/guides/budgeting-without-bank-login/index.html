Budgeting on Mac without linking your bank

Updated 2026-11-22

Keel is a free, open-source budgeting and planning app for the Mac that works from the statements you download, so it never needs your bank login and your data never leaves your Mac. YNAB, Monarch Money and Copilot Money usually connect to your accounts through services such as Plaid, keep your data in the cloud, and cost about $100–110 a year.

The trade-off

Linked accounts update by themselves; statements you download do not. Keel makes importing quick: drop files anywhere in the app, or save them to a ~/Finance-Inbox folder and they are picked up. In exchange, no third party holds a login to your bank, and nothing about your money is stored on someone else's server. Some connected apps also lose their links to smaller banks and credit unions, which then need re-linking.

If you want automatic syncing on your phone and are comfortable linking accounts, the subscription apps do that well. If you want privacy, a one-time setup and no monthly fee, Keel is built for that.

What you get

  • Now: this month against a usual month, a month-end forecast, what you can still spend per day, and a cash timeline to past your next payday.
  • Review: each closed month's income, spending, savings and net worth, with categories compared to usual.
  • Ledger: every transaction, a calendar of each day with upcoming bills and paydays, and edits that survive every re-import.
  • Plan: retirement and market-crash simulations on data back to 1871, allocation, backtests, mortgage and rent-or-buy.
  • Alerts: overspending pace, a paycheck that didn't arrive, cash below your floor, bills this week.
  • For agents: a keel command and an MCP server with 20 tools, so Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can check balances, categorise and plan.

FAQ

Is there a budgeting app that doesn't need my bank login?

Yes. Keel works from the statements you download from your bank, so it never asks for your bank credentials.

Is Keel a free YNAB alternative?

For Mac users, yes. It covers budgeting, cash forecasting and retirement planning for free, without linking your accounts.

Does Keel work on iPhone?

No. Keel is a Mac app.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your Mac.
